# Air Map and Air Data (ADS-B, UAT and FIS-B)

Two panels in the app: **Air Map** (aircraft, trails, weather radar on a map) and **Air Data** (the text: METAR, TAF,
PIREP, SIGMET, AIRMET, NOTAM/TFR, alerts, receiver health). They share one `AviationModel`
(`App/Shared/Aviation/AviationModel.swift`) and one `AviationPicture` (core), so a receiver feeds both.

## Sources

| Source | What it gives | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Demo sky | Made-up aircraft covering every icon class, storms, sample reports and alerts. Deterministic. | nothing |
| 1090 MHz ADS-B | Airliners and most other aircraft: position (CPR), altitude, speed, heading, vertical rate, callsign, squawk, emitter category, emergencies | RTL-SDR + 1090 MHz antenna |
| 978 MHz UAT + FIS-B | US general aviation, TIS-B/ADS-R traffic, ground stations, **NEXRAD radar** and **text products** uplinked by FIS-B ground stations | RTL-SDR + 978 MHz antenna |

One dongle tunes one band at a time. With two dongles both sources run side by side; with one, pick a source. A dongle can
only be used by one program (or one panel of this app) at a time, so stop the Scanner before starting a live source.

The decoders are the embedded `Packages/SwiftRTLSDR` package (`RTLSDRDecoders`). It is currently synced to the head of
upstream draft PR #2 (`6bf6398`). Re-sync when that PR changes or merges. `UATPipeline` and `ModeSPipeline` in core adapt
the driver's frames to `AircraftReport`/`RadarBlock`/`AviationMessage` so the rest of the app never sees decoder types.

## What the map shows

- **Icons.** 20 kinds, painted as layered vector shapes (shadow, shared outline, tones, glass, highlight, discs, lines)
  and generated by `script/generate_aircraft_icons.py` into `Aviation/AircraftIconData.swift`. `docs/aircraft-icons.png` is
  the preview sheet. The kind comes from the ADS-B/UAT emitter category (light, small, large, high-vortex large, heavy,
  high-performance, rotorcraft, glider, balloon, parachutist, ultralight, UAV, space vehicle, surface emergency/service,
  obstacles); when a category is missing it is inferred from callsign, speed and altitude, and a "small" aircraft that
  flies fast or high is drawn as a business jet.
- **Color = altitude.** `AltitudeColorScale` runs warm at the ground (red/orange) through yellow, green, cyan and blue to
  violet at the flight levels (stops at 0, 1,000, 3,000, 6,000, 10,000, 15,000, 20,000, 30,000, 40,000 ft). Aircraft on the
  ground are drawn in a neutral tone. Emergency squawks (7500, 7600, 7700) ring the icon and raise an alert.
- **Trails.** Every aircraft keeps a position history (`TrackHistory`, thinned by distance/time/altitude change). The trail is
  drawn as short solid segments, each colored by the altitude at that point, so climbs and descents read as a gradient.
  Gaps and impossible jumps break the trail instead of drawing a straight line across the map.
- **Radar.** FIS-B NEXRAD blocks are assembled into a mosaic (`RadarMosaic`) and rastered to an image aligned to the map's
  Web Mercator grid (`RadarRasterizer`). Levels 0 and 1 are transparent; 2-7 run green to magenta. The map's own aircraft
  layer is a SwiftUI `Canvas` positioned with `MapViewport` (plain arithmetic per frame) rather than per-point MapKit calls.
- Positions between reports are dead-reckoned from speed and heading, and expire after their timeout.

## Air Data

Messages go through `AviationMessageFeed`: repeated broadcasts of the same report become one row with a count; the feed
filters by kind, severity and text, and can show only the latest report per station. METAR text is decoded
(`METARDecoder`: wind, visibility, weather, sky cover, temperature, altimeter) and classified VFR/MVFR/IFR/LIFR from
ceiling and visibility. The airport weather board is built from those reports. **METAR flags on the map are not drawn yet**:
that needs an airport-coordinate dataset the project does not have.

## Testing

Core tests (`ADSB1090PipelineTests`, `UAT978PipelineTests`) use published test frames: the Mode S frames of "The 1090 MHz
Riddle", and dump978's sample recording with its decoded values as the oracle (aircraft position/altitude/speed/track,
squawk, a winds-aloft uplink, a NEXRAD uplink of 41 records). Nothing here has been checked over the air with an antenna.
